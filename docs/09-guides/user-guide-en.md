# User guide — HR Management System

For employees, managers and HR staff. The menu shows only what your permissions allow, so some sections below may not appear for you.

## 1. Signing in and your account

- **Sign in** with your username and password. If two-step verification is on, enter the code from your authenticator app.
- **First sign-in:** you may be asked to change your password and to set up two-step verification. Keep the recovery codes in a safe place.
- **Locked account:** the account locks after several wrong attempts and unlocks by itself after a short time. If you lost your verification phone, ask the system administrator to reset it.
- **Language:** switch between Arabic and English at the top of the page.
- **My account** (top menu): change your password, manage two-step verification, and see or end your open sessions.
- **Sign out** from the top menu. Always sign out on shared devices.
- **On a phone:** add the system to your home screen from the browser menu, and it works like an app.

## 2. Home, search and notifications

- **Home** shows cards for your role, for example my attendance today, my leave balance, or requests waiting for me.
- **Search** (top bar) finds employees, contracts, candidates and vacancies. You only see results you are allowed to see.
- **Notifications** (bell icon): you can turn on browser notifications there. On iPhone, add the system to the home screen first.
- **Approvals** lists every request waiting for your decision: leave, attendance corrections, payroll, separations and job offers. When you reject a request, give a clear reason.

## 3. My profile

My profile shows your data, documents, contracts, attendance and leave. If something is wrong, ask HR to correct it.

## 4. Attendance

1. Open **Attendance**.
2. **First time only:** register your device under *My attendance devices*. You can use your fingerprint, face or device PIN. The check happens on your device; the system never stores fingerprints.
3. Press **Check in** when you start work and **Check out** when you finish.
4. You must be on an authorised company network. If you see *Not on an authorised company network* while in the office, tell HR.
5. **Forgot to punch?** Send a **correction request** from your day list, with a reason. Your manager approves it.

**Managers and HR:** use **Attendance review** to follow absences and lateness and to confirm or resolve absences. Every change is recorded in the audit log.

## 5. Leave

1. Open **Leave** to see your balances for each leave type.
2. Press **Request leave** and choose the type and dates. Public holidays and weekly days off are not counted.
3. Attach a document if the leave type needs one, for example a medical certificate for sick leave.
4. Follow the status of your request on the same page. You can cancel it before it is approved.
5. **Short permissions** (hours) are requested on the same page, if your company uses them.

**HR:** **Leave management** handles requests, balances and balance adjustments (each with a reason), and leave types and their rules.

## 6. My payslips

Each month's payslip appears after payroll is approved. You can print it or save it as PDF from the browser.

## 7. Contracts

- **Employees** see their contract in their profile and can sign it electronically when it is sent to them.
- **HR:**
  1. Go to **Contracts → New contract**, choose the employee and the template, and review the text.
  2. Send it for signature.
  3. Once both parties have signed, the contract is in force and its text cannot change. Any later change is made as a new version.
  4. If the contract was signed on paper, upload the signed copy after sending it for signature.
- Nobody can prepare or change their own contract.

## 8. Payroll (finance and HR)

1. Go to **Payroll → New run** and choose the company and month.
2. Salaries are calculated from compensation, attendance and leave.
3. Add manual items, such as bonuses or deductions, with a reason. You cannot add items to your own pay.
4. **Send the run for approval.** Whoever prepared the run or added items to it cannot approve it. Someone else must approve.
5. After approval the run is locked, payslips appear for employees, and payment is recorded.

## 9. Separation

Requests are made under **Separation**: resignation, termination or end of term. The request follows the approval route, and the system calculates the final settlement. After the last working day the employee's account closes automatically. The records stay with their company.

## 10. Recruitment

1. Create the vacancy.
2. Add candidates and their files, such as a CV.
3. Move candidates through the stages and schedule interviews with interviewers.
4. Create the offer, then turn the accepted candidate into an employee in one step.

**Interviewers** see the candidate's name and stage and record only their own feedback. They do not see contact details or internal notes.

## 11. Commitment evaluation

The score is calculated automatically from attendance (attendance rate, punctuality, no absences and so on). There is no performance appraisal, goal setting or self-assessment. Employees see their own score if the company allows it.

## 12. Reports

1. Open **Reports** and choose a dataset, columns and filters.
2. Export to Excel or CSV, or print.
3. Save a report and schedule it daily, weekly or monthly. The file arrives in your notifications and is kept for 30 days.

Sensitive columns, such as salary and ID numbers, appear only for people with that permission. Every export is recorded.

## 13. Privacy

- Each person sees only their own scope: themselves, their department, or their company.
- Every view or change of sensitive data is recorded in the audit log.
- Do not send employee data outside the system unless it is needed, and never share your password.
- The system does not use GPS location and does not store fingerprints.

## 14. Common problems

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| I cannot check in | Make sure you are on the company network and your device is registered. A new device must be registered again |
| No notifications | Turn them on from the bell icon and allow them in your browser settings. They are always available inside the system |
| I cannot see a section | You do not have the permission for it. Ask HR or the system administrator |
| My account is locked | Wait 15 minutes, or ask the administrator to unlock it |
